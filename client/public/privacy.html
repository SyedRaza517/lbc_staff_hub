Privacy Policy

London Brookes College — Staff Hub · Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how the LBC Staff Hub application ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores and protects personal data when you use it. The app is provided by London Brookes College ("the College") for use by its staff and administrators. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is London Brookes College. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at [privacy@londonbrookescollege.co.uk].

2. What data we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, job title and department.
  • Authentication data — a securely hashed password and, where enabled, two‑step (authenticator) verification data.
  • Attendance & leave — daily check‑in/out times, daily summaries, holiday balances and leave requests.
  • Documents & notifications — documents shared with you and in‑app notifications.
  • Administrative records (staff/admin users only) — where you hold an administrator role, the app processes student records (names, reference numbers, college email addresses), attendance registers, assessment marks and pastoral (PAT) interaction notes, for the purpose of running the College's programmes.
  • Device data — if you enable push notifications, a device notification token; and standard technical data needed to operate the service securely.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the app's features: sign‑in, attendance, leave management, documents, notifications, and (for administrators) student attendance and assessment management.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To send you notifications relevant to your account (e.g. leave decisions).

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the basis of the College's legitimate interests in administering its staff and educational programmes, and to perform our contract with you as a member of staff. Special‑category or sensitive information is only processed where necessary and with appropriate safeguards.

5. How your data is stored and protected

  • Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase within the European Union.
  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • Access is protected by signed session tokens, role‑based access control and optional two‑step verification.

6. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that host and operate the app on our behalf, under appropriate data‑protection terms:

  • Supabase — database hosting (EU).
  • Render — application/API hosting.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — only if push notifications are enabled, to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Email is sent via our configured email provider to deliver account and password‑reset messages.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as you hold an account with the College, and delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed, or when you delete your account (see below), subject to any legal retention obligations.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact [privacy@londonbrookescollege.co.uk]. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your own account and personal data from within the app: Staff App → More → Delete my account. This removes your profile, check‑in history, leave requests, balance adjustments, notifications and any outstanding password‑reset links. The action requires your password (and your authenticator code if two‑step verification is on) and cannot be undone.

10. Children

The app is intended for College staff and administrators. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children through this app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
